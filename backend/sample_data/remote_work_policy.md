# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for hybrid remote work. Roles that require on-site equipment are excluded.

## Schedule
Hybrid employees work from the office at least two days per week. Team leads define the shared office days for their teams. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for remote work. Employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 500 USD. Internet costs are reimbursed up to 50 USD per month.

## Security requirements
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited. Company data must never be stored on personal devices.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Employees must be reachable on Teams during core hours.
