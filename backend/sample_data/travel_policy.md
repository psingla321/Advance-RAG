# Travel and Expense Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors who travel on company business. Managers must approve all business travel before booking.

## Booking
Employees must book flights and hotels through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than six hours with director approval.

## Hotel limits
The nightly hotel limit is 200 USD in standard cities and 300 USD in high-cost cities such as London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich. Stays above the limit require written approval from a vice president.

## Meals and per diem
The daily meal per diem is 75 USD for domestic travel and 90 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client entertainment event.

## Reimbursement
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after finance approval. Late reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.
