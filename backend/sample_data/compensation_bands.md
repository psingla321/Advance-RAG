# Compensation Bands (HR Confidential)

## Engineering bands
Software Engineer I base salary band is 95,000 to 120,000 USD. Software Engineer II base salary band is 120,000 to 150,000 USD. Senior Software Engineer base salary band is 150,000 to 190,000 USD.

## Bonus targets
The annual bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for individual contributors and 15 percent for managers. Bonus payouts are determined by company and individual performance.

## Review cycle
Compensation reviews take place every April. Off-cycle adjustments require approval from the HR business partner and the department head.
